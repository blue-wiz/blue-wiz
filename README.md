## Featured projects

### ⚽ [ShotIQ](https://github.com/blue-wiz/shotiq): expected-goals (xG) model
How likely was that shot to be a goal? The model is trained on **38,318 shots** from the 2015/16 big-five European leagues and tested on a league it never saw. It comes **within 3% of StatsBomb's commercial xG** and is well calibrated, predicting 929 goals against 945 actual.

**Research extension:** a causal analysis (double machine learning) showing that teams create shots worth **18% more xG when leading**, because of extra space and counter-attacks.

`Python` `scikit-learn` `DoubleML` `pandas` `pytest`

### 🛒 [RetailPulse](https://github.com/blue-wiz/retailpulse): customer and revenue analytics
A PostgreSQL star schema, SQL analysis (RFM segmentation, cohort retention, Pareto curve), and a next-quarter spend model, all built on **541,909 transactions** from a UK online retailer. Key finding: the top 23% of customers bring in **68% of revenue**.

`SQL` `PostgreSQL` `Python` `scikit-learn`

### 💳 [FraudLens](https://github.com/blue-wiz/Fraud-Detection): card-fraud detection
Fraud models judged on **money saved**, not accuracy, using 284,807 card transactions with a time-based split and a cost model that charges for every manual review. The best model avoids **62% of fraud losses** after review costs, and a simpler, explainable model does almost as well.

`Python` `scikit-learn` `Imbalanced data` `pytest`

### ⚡ [GridFlow](https://github.com/blue-wiz/gridflow): energy data pipeline
A scheduled ELT pipeline that loads global energy and CO₂ data every week. It uses idempotent loads, dbt models and **26 data tests**, and runs on GitHub Actions. It shows the UK's low-carbon share of electricity rising from 23% to 65%, the **2nd-fastest rise of 108 countries**.

`dbt` `PostgreSQL` `Python` `GitHub Actions` `Docker`
